PENCILWISE - learn to draw, and a Bible quiz, on iPad and iPhone (works offline)

START PENCILWISE ON THE PC
Double-click "Start Pencilwise" in this folder. A black window opens, and after about 10 seconds it shows:
  - an address for the iPads and iPhones (https://....trycloudflare.com)
  - the words for a live game (the part before .trycloudflare.com)
Keep the window open while you install or play a live game. Close it to stop.
(It needs Python and cloudflared on the PC, which are already set up.)

PUT IT ON AN IPAD OR IPHONE
1. Back up everyone in the old Pencilwise icon first (see BACKUPS below).
2. Start Pencilwise on the PC, as above.
3. On the iPad or iPhone, open the address in Safari and wait for the lessons to appear.
4. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
5. Open Pencilwise from the new icon and restore your backup file.
From then on it works with no internet at all. Each new address makes a new icon, so always back up first.

FAMILY COMPETITION (Bible quiz tab)
- On this iPad or iPhone: everyone takes turns on one device.
- Live on phones and iPads: start Pencilwise on the PC, type its words on each device,
  start a game on one device and join with the 4-digit game number on the others.
  Scores update on every device. Needs the internet and the window open on the PC.
- Phones and iPads, no internet: one device starts a match and shows a match code.
  The others join with that code and choose different team colours. Each device plays as one team.
  At the end, swap the result codes to see the winner. Good for travelling.
- Gleaning: after a wrong answer the next team can glean the question for 5 points, as Ruth gleaned in the field.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Always open it from the Home Screen icon. Drawings and progress are stored inside that icon on that device.
- Deleting the icon deletes that device's drawings and progress.
- Names, progress and drawings never leave the device, except the player names in a live game, which pass through your own PC.
- Apple Pencil: press harder for darker lines, tilt to shade. Once a Pencil touches the screen, palms are ignored.
- Two-finger tap = undo. Three-finger tap = redo.

BACKUPS
- On the "Who is drawing today?" screen, tap "Back up everyone". Or open Gallery and back up one person.
- Tap "Save backup file", choose "Save to Files", pick "On My iPad" (or iPhone) or "iCloud Drive", then tap Save.
- To bring work back: on the first screen tap "Restore from a backup file" and choose the backup.
- Backups include the family scoreboard and saved games. Restoring the same backup twice is safe.

UPDATING LATER
If any file is changed, also change the VERSION line at the top of sw.js.
